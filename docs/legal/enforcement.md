# Enforcement

NodOS is free and it stays free. If you find someone selling it, wrapping it in
a paid product or service, charging for support or hosting of it, or running
ads or paywalls around it, tell us.

What happens next: we will happily file a takedown with whoever hosts or
distributes it. That can mean the code host, the app store or marketplace, the
web host, the payment processor, the search engine, or all of them. We will
also contact the seller first when that is likely to settle it faster.

## How to report

Open a private security advisory or contact the maintainers through the
repository, and include:

- the link to the listing, site or repository,
- what is being charged for (a screenshot helps),
- anything showing it is NodOS or based on NodOS, such as the name, the logo,
  copied source files or identical build output.

## What is fine

Forks, patches and your own builds shared for free are welcome, as long as they
follow the [license](../../LICENSE). Using NodOS to manage your own or your
employer's servers is fine. Voluntary tips with nothing offered in return are
not something we chase, but nothing may be gated behind them.

This page states intent and is not legal advice.
